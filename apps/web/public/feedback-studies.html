Portal Feedback studies

A little clarity after every action.

Ten treatments for saved, deleted and failed actions. Replay the motion, try each result and compare both themes.

Suggested starting point Use Quiet confirmation for general actions, Inline result beside a form, and Activity entry when someone needs a lasting record. Keep failures visible and provide Try again. Offer Undo only when the application can restore the deleted item.

Interactive mockups. Undo and retry affect these previews. Success clears after seven idle seconds when “Keep previews visible” is off. Failures stay visible until dismissed.
