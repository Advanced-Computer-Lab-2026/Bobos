## Shared visual constraints

The supplied Overview was opened after the existing sign-in and its calendar advanced and returned before design work. Light and dark were compared at the same 1440 × 1000 viewport. The final studio captures keep the 224-pixel sidebar and 48-pixel top bar. Page title sizes, native buttons, neutral canvas, term treatment and course-specific artwork come from the host. The connected React app has its own existing shell; the adapters leave that shell unchanged rather than replacing it with the studio shell.

Result counts and credit totals serve the task; no decorative hero, KPI strip, new logo or design-studio toolbar was added. Mobile cards retain labels and actionable links, and long blocker text wraps. Dense ledgers remain horizontally scrollable inside their table region where appropriate, not by widening the page. Empty results offer a clear-filters path. Selected-record views have explicit in-app return actions.

## Motion and artwork

`src/motion.js` contains exposed poses and holds, not a continuously floating illustration. Folder cover and paper paths change independently at 0, 100, 190, 290, 420, 600 and 850 ms, then rest. The native envelope strip uses held frames 0, 1, 3, 5, 7 and 9 and stops open. The existing conversation outline receives three tiny path variations on record selection, then rests. Replay follows a selected student/request or an explicit form opening—not an endless attention animation. Detached nodes cancel pending exposures through an instance token.

The native course timetable drawings are retained, including their course-specific movement. The existing binder and sun/moon greeting were studied in the host; Overview itself remains Original. The authored small record folder is not a replacement for the approved extra-hours art: `assets/extra-hours-folder.svg` is copied unchanged and used specifically where credit approval/settlement constraints are explained. Connected React messaging reuses the supplied conversation paths with short, stepped local ink movement and a quiet resting state.

`prefers-reduced-motion` prevents new exposed-pose playback and disables owned CSS animation/transition. The still drawing and its semantic text remain. Animated drawings are `aria-hidden`; they do not carry the only explanation of a blocker, decision or success. Native dialogs provide keyboard focus containment, Escape cancellation when not saving, and focus return.
