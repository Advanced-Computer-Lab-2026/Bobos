THE MOTION, IN FOUR BEATS

Lift. Arc. Catch. Return.

A continuous, hand-placed arc with small changes in the page contour. The paper stays behind the button until it rises above the top edge.

01
Pick up the sheet

The sheet peeks out near the final “t” and climbs in short, unequal steps.

02
Toss it into the bin

A quick diagonal climb, a soft turn at the peak, then a precise catch in the little bin.

03
Show Deleted

The button turns muted coral with an undo arrow on the left and only Deleted as its label.

04
Press again to undo

The paper returns along the same arc; cream returns only after it disappears behind the right edge.

Integration detail: This is a visual demo; no file is deleted. In your real Portal, Undo must restore the actual item—not just the button. Connect it to a reversible delete (soft delete, delayed commit or a server-side restore action), and only show Deleted after the deletion succeeds.
